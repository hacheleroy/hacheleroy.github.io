About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL, working with Prof. Romain Fleury on wavefront shaping for ultrasound micromanipulation in scattering media.

My research lies at the intersection of acoustics, wave physics, signal processing, and biomedical ultrasound. I am interested in using waves both to image and control complex systems.

During my PhD at ESPCI Paris – PSL, advised by Dr. Mathieu Pernot and Dr. Guillaume Goudot, I developed volumetric Doppler and ultrasound localization microscopy methods for vascular imaging. Since July 2026, my work at EPFL has focused on recovering precise acoustic control through scattering environments.

Research interests

  • Wavefront shaping. Controlling acoustic propagation through strongly scattering media by measuring and shaping complex wavefronts.
  • Ultrasound micromanipulation. Designing focused acoustic fields for precise, contactless manipulation of microscopic objects in complex environments.
  • Volumetric vascular imaging. Developing fast 3D and 4D ultrasound methods to measure vascular flow with row-column arrays and wide-field probes.
  • Super-resolution ultrasound. Ultrasound localization microscopy, Doppler estimation, SVD filtering, and beamforming grounded in wave physics.
